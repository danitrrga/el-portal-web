this plan writes only here, never to the shared file). Every string in the
`manifesto` namespace was checked against `SPANISH-VOICE.md`'s four

| manifesto.hero.headline | A method for becoming yourself. | Un método para llegar a ser quien eres. | The English compresses "a method" and "becoming yourself" into one clean noun phrase with no verb; the Spanish needs an explicit infinitive clause ("para llegar a ser quien eres") to read naturally under the tú register, which is longer and slightly less aphoristic than the English original — the meaning survives, the compression does not. | 2 (rhythm) |
| manifesto.hero.body | The system gives you the structure. You bring the will. | El sistema te da la estructura. Aportas la voluntad. | English states both actors as explicit paired subjects ("The system gives" / "You bring"), a visible two-actor symmetry. The tú register bans a written subject pronoun, so the second clause carries its actor only in the verb ending ("Aportas"); the sentence is still correct and idiomatic, but the overt subject-for-subject parallelism that makes the English couplet land is not reproducible without breaking the register rule. | 2 (rhythm) |
| manifesto.principles[1].body | ...planning collapses into either daydreaming or grind. | ...la planificación se derrumba, ya sea en ensoñación o en agobio. | "Grind" is a single blunt monosyllable carrying a specific idiom (the daily grind — monotonous, wearing toil) that lands hard against "daydreaming." "Agobio" (being overwhelmed/burdened) is the closest natural Spanish noun but is longer, softer-sounding, and denotes stress rather than the specific grinding-toil image; the contrast survives, the percussive one-word punch does not. | 2 (rhythm) |
| manifesto.principles[3].body | ...including correlations, recurrences, and ripples. | ...correlaciones, recurrencias y ondas expansivas. | "Ripples" is one compact word evoking a gentle, spreading physical metaphor. The comparison survives; the terse, aphoristic snap of the English original does not. | 2 (rhythm) |
