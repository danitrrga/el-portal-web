# Translation Flags — Plan 07-06 (Hero, VCDSection, McpIntegrationSection)

Per-plan register file (see `TRANSLATION-FLAGS.md`'s ownership contract —
this plan writes only here, never to the shared file). Every string this
plan translated in the `home` namespace (`hero.*`, `vcd.*`, `mcpIntro.*`) was
checked against `SPANISH-VOICE.md`'s four wordplay triggers, not sampled.
Four rows fired; the rest translate clean.

| Key | English | Spanish shipped | What was lost | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| home.hero.headline | The final operating system for high-performers. | El sistema operativo definitivo para el alto rendimiento. | "High-performers" is a specific identity label in English productivity culture — it names a *type of person*, not a level of performance. Spanish has no equally compact nominal equivalent that names the person rather than the domain, so the shipped line targets "high performance" (the pursuit) rather than "high-performers" (the people who pursue it). The overall claim still lands, but the identity-label snap of the English original — the site is *for people like you*, not *for the domain of high performance* — is softened. | 3 (no clean equivalent) |
| home.vcd.subheading | Versions plan identity. Cycles run focus. Days carry the score. | Las Versiones planifican identidad. Los Ciclos concentran el enfoque. Los Días llevan la puntuación. | The English is three matched three-word clauses (subject-verb-object, no articles) — a percussive, almost telegraphic cadence that visually mirrors the "three horizons" concept above it. Spanish requires an article before each subject ("Las Versiones", "Los Ciclos", "Los Días"), which breaks the exact three-word symmetry into four- and five-word clauses; the parallel meaning survives, the telegraphic one-beat-per-clause rhythm does not. | 2 (rhythm) |
| home.vcd.captions[2].body | Atomic unit. Weighted habits roll into one number. | Unidad atómica. Los hábitos ponderados se combinan en un número. | "Roll into" carries a physical, almost snowball-like accumulation image — small weighted pieces gathering momentum into a single total. "Se combinan en" (combine into) is the natural Spanish rendering but is a flat, mechanical verb with no motion or accumulation imagery; the meaning (many habits become one score) survives, the rolling-momentum picture does not. | 1 (idiom/double meaning) |
| home.mcpIntro.body | Connect Claude, Cursor, or any MCP-compatible agent. It reads habits, cycles, and trends. Writes back updates. The list's factual content survives; its single-language fluency does not. | 3 (no clean equivalent) |
