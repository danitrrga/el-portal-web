<objective>
Extract every user-facing string from `/features` — at 970 lines the largest
single surface on the site — into the English catalogue, changing nothing a
visitor can see.

Purpose: `/features` and `/changelog` are the only two surfaces where extraction
and translation together would produce an unreviewable diff, so they are split
across two waves. This plan does the mechanical half: move roughly ninety strings
out of five deep-dive section components into a structured catalogue, and prove
English is untouched. Plan 07-13 then writes `src/messages/es/features.json` as a
pure JSON write, touching no component.

The split is what makes the Spanish translation reviewable: 07-13's diff is one
file of prose, with no interleaved JSX churn to read past.

The structure chosen here is therefore a contract, not an implementation detail.
If the English catalogue mirrors the page's real section structure, 07-13 is a
translation job; if it flattens everything into a bag of keys, 07-13 becomes an
archaeology job.

Output: a complete `features` English namespace and an unchanged English page.
</objective>
