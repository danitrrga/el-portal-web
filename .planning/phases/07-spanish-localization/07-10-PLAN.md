<objective>
Translate `/privacy` and `/terms` (465 lines combined) and add the
English-governs notice both pages need.

Purpose: D-07 is locked — translate both, and state visibly that English is
authoritative in case of discrepancy. That is standard practice, it keeps content
parity, and it removes the meaning risk that made legal translation an open
question in research §6. Spanish legal copy is for comprehension; English
governs.

D-08 flags both pages for human read-through before merge. With
`human_verify_mode: end-of-phase` that read-through is collected as a
`<human-check>` rather than as a blocking checkpoint, and it is listed here so
the end-of-phase verification cannot lose it.

The register applies here too. `07-RESEARCH.md` §7 rule 6 is explicit: never
`usted`, "not as a fallback, not for legal copy. One register across the whole
site." Legal Spanish drifts formal by default, so this is the surface where the
CI register gate earns its keep.

Output: a `legal` namespace in both locales, with the authority notice rendered
on both pages in both languages.
</objective>
