<objective>
Translate `/pricing` (506 lines) — tiers, feature lists, comparison table and FAQ
— while leaving every price, currency symbol and billing period untouched.

Purpose: locale-specific pricing and currency are an explicit non-goal of this
phase (ROADMAP.md Non-goals). The Spanish page sells the same product at the same
price in the same currency; only the words around the numbers change.

This page carries the phase's single highest layout risk. The plan badge at
`pricing/page.tsx:424-434` is `whitespace-nowrap`, absolutely centred with
`left-1/2 -translate-x-1/2`, and has **no `max-width`**. It grows symmetrically
from the centre with no ellipsis fallback, so a Spanish badge string that is too
long does not truncate — it spills out of the card in both directions. 07-UI-SPEC
rates it High and states that truncation is not an acceptable fallback here
because a badge broken mid-word reads as a bug.

The page also carries KU-4: the comparison table already escapes its
`overflow-hidden` wrapper by 77px at 320px in English.

Output: a `pricing` namespace in both locales, with the badge measured rather
than eyeballed.
</objective>

design-owner finding in deferred-items.md rather than silencing the sweep.
