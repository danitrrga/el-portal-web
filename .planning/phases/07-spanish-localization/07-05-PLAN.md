<objective>
Translate `/manifesto` first, and make its register the gate the rest of the
phase passes through.

Purpose: D-06 is explicit — `/manifesto` is the quality bar, not the last file.
It is 244 lines of philosophical second-person prose and the surface the entire
`tu` register decision was made for. Its English hook, *"A method for becoming
yourself"*, is what exposed the first (fully impersonal) register as reading
institutional: `un metodo para llegar a ser uno mismo` is grammatically fine and
emotionally wrong for a product about personal identity. Getting this page right
before ~350 more strings are written is the difference between one correction and
a phase-wide rewrite.

This plan also owns the glyph verification 07-UI-SPEC requires and nobody else
can do earlier: `.display` is uppercase Special Gothic Expanded One with
`line-height: 1.02`, tuned against unaccented English capitals. Accented
uppercase sits above cap height with near-zero slack. If the font subset is
missing those glyphs or the accents clip, that is cheapest to discover now and
most expensive to discover in Wave 4.

Output: a fully translated `/manifesto`, a verified glyph rendering path, and an
approved register.
</objective>

  <action>
Render-check, do not reason about, whether Special Gothic Expanded One serves
Spanish accented capitals under the `.display` utility.

Special Gothic Expanded One is loaded with `subsets: ["latin"]`. Google Fonts'
"latin" subset conventionally includes the accented Latin-1 range, which is
distinct from "latin-ext" — but 07-UI-SPEC states plainly that this was never
verified by rendering in this repo and that the executor must check it rather
than inherit the assumption.

Against the running production build, render a probe string containing every
Spanish accented capital and the inverted question mark — acute accents on A, E,
I, O and U, the tilde on N, and the opening inverted question mark — inside a
`.display` element, at both the mobile-floor and the desktop-ceiling clamp
values. Use the manifesto H1 slot or a temporary scratch element; either way,
remove the probe before finishing. It must not ship.

Confirm two separate things in DevTools:
  1. No tofu or `.notdef` boxes for any accented capital or for the inverted
     question mark. Check the computed font family actually resolved to Special
     Gothic Expanded One and did not silently fall back to `Arial Black`, which
     would hide a missing glyph behind a glyph that exists.
  2. No vertical clipping of the accent marks. An accent sits above cap height,
     and a `line-height: 1.02` box tuned for unaccented English capitals has
     near-zero slack.

If glyphs are missing, add `"latin-ext"` to that font's `subsets` array in
`src/app/[locale]/layout.tsx`. That is cheap and changes nothing about English
rendering.

If accents are visually clipped, the fix is PER-INSTANCE: add vertical padding or
margin to the specific heading's wrapper element. Do NOT raise the shared
`.display` `line-height: 1.02`, and do not touch any per-component
`leading-[1.05]` or `leading-[1.1]`. Those are Phase 5 tuned values shared with
English, and changing them changes English's approved rendering too — which is
the frozen-desktop change RESP-08 forbids.

Record the outcome either way. "Checked, no change needed" is a result this phase
needs written down; every later plan writing Spanish display headings is relying
on it.
