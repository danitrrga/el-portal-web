<objective>
Translate `/manifesto` first, and make its register the gate the rest of the
phase passes through.

Purpose: D-06 is explicit — `/manifesto` is the quality bar, not the last file.
It is 244 lines of philosophical second-person prose and the surface the entire
`tu` register decision was made for. Its English hook, *"A method for becoming
yourself"* (confirmed verbatim at `manifesto/page.tsx:211`), is what exposed the
first (fully impersonal) register as reading institutional:
`un metodo para llegar a ser uno mismo` is grammatically fine and emotionally
wrong for a product about personal identity. Getting this page right before ~350
more strings are written is the difference between one correction and a
phase-wide rewrite.

This plan also owns the glyph verification nobody else can do earlier, and the
cross-AI review sharpened why it matters: `07-PATTERNS.md` confirms by direct
read that `src/app/layout.tsx` loads ALL FOUR fonts with `subsets: ["latin"]`
only — this repo has never loaded `latin-ext` for anything. Latin-Extended
coverage is per-family and is not inherited from Inter, so `Á É Í Ó Ú Ñ` in
Special Gothic Expanded One is a genuine open question rather than a formality.
`.display` is uppercase with `line-height: 1.02`, tuned against unaccented
English capitals, so an accent sitting above cap height has near-zero slack.

Output: a fully translated `/manifesto`, a scripted glyph verdict written into
`SPANISH-VOICE.md`, and an approved register.
</objective>
