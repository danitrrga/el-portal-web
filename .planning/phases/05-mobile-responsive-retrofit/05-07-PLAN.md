**Why the `md:` half is safe.** `md:text-[clamp(42px,4.2vw,58px)]` is character-identical to
today's inline expression, and Tailwind's `md:` compiles to `@media (width >= 48rem)`. Every
viewport at or above 768px therefore computes the same font-size it computes now: 42px at
768, 43.008px at 1024, 58px at 1440. The desktop design is frozen by construction, not by
inspection.

**Why the mobile half is what it is.** Derived with RESPONSIVE.md's own formula for
26px at 320px → 42px at 768px, so the mobile curve meets the `md:` restore point
continuously (at 767px it computes ≈41.96px, at 768px exactly 42px — no visible step):
slope `v = 100 × (42 − 26) / (768 − 320) = 3.571vw`;
intercept `r = (320×42 − 768×26) / (320 − 768) = 14.571px = 0.911rem`.
It carries the mandatory `+ Nrem` term (so it still scales at 400% zoom, WCAG 1.4.4) and its
ratio is `42 / 26 = 1.62`, inside the `≤ 2.5` accessible ceiling and inside the preferred
`≤ 2`. At 390px it computes ≈28.5px, which puts the `PERFORMERS.` token at roughly 235px
inside a 342px box.

**Change nothing else.** Keep `.display`, `text-balance`, `max-w-4xl`, `mt-8`, `lg:mt-16`,
`leading-[1.05]`, and both `color` values. Do not convert any other inline clamp in either
file (see the scope fence for the full list). Do not touch the hardcoded hex / token
question. Do not reflow or reformat unrelated JSX.
