No marketing-only term coinages were needed for `/manifesto`. Every El
Portal product noun the page uses (Identity, Habit/Habits, Version, Cycle,
Day, System, Friction, Patterns, Correlations, Signals, Sprint) resolves
directly through `src/messages/GLOSSARY.md` — including "sprint," which the
app's own catalogue keeps as an untranslated lowercase loanword
(`Número de sprints`, `settings.general.sprintLength`), matched here.
Remaining vocabulary (companion, coach, obstacle, discipline, boot,
shutdown, recurrence, ripple, and so on) is general philosophical prose, not
an App-specific product noun, and was translated directly rather than
coined.
